The ground around Carbon Hill, AL

Walker County is old coal country, crossed by I-22 and the Mulberry Fork of the Black Warrior River, with the southern arm of Lewis Smith Lake reaching into its north-east corner.

Steep hollows, ridge lines and reclaimed mine ground, with lake lots along the Smith Lake side and a lot of former mine property now being sold off in tracts.

What we get called for here

Steep ground, old mine property being sold off in tracts, and lake lots that need an access road before anything else can happen. Access is usually the first problem to solve in this county, not the last.

Carbon Hill — questions we get asked

How far out are you booking?

It moves with the weather and what's already on the schedule. Call or text and we'll tell you honestly where you'd fall instead of promising a date we can't hold.

Can you get a machine to my property?

Usually. A tracked machine gets into places a wheeled one can't, but it still arrives on a trailer, so the gate, the bridge and the first hundred feet of drive matter. Tell us about the access when you call.

Do you work small properties?

Yes. Half an acre behind a house is a normal job. Very small jobs carry a minimum to cover getting the equipment there and back, and we'll tell you that number up front.

Do you actually come out to Carbon Hill?

Yes. Carbon Hill is in Walker County and it's inside our normal working area out of Berry. There's no travel surcharge for being in the eight counties we cover.

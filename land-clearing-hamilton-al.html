The ground around Hamilton, AL

Marion County lies along the Mississippi state line in the north-west of Alabama, crossed by US-78 and I-22, with the Buttahatchee River running through the south of the county.

Hill country with a lot of standing timber, small pasture tracts and river bottom. Poultry houses and cattle are common, and so is land that has been in timber long enough to need real clearing rather than mulching.

What we get called for here

Timber tracts, poultry and cattle ground, and river bottom that needs the understory taken out. A lot of the work here is heavy enough that it's a clearing job rather than a mulching job, and we'll say so.

Straight Answers

Hamilton — questions we get asked

Is mulching or clearing cheaper?

Mulching, almost always — one machine instead of three and nothing to haul. Clearing costs more because the stumps come out and the debris leaves. See the cost guide for how each is priced.

Do you take on commercial work?

Yes — commercial lots, right of way, laydown yards and site prep alongside the residential and rural work.

Do you charge to come look at it?

No. Estimates are free. If you're not local we can go over the property with you on satellite and get close before anybody drives anywhere.

Do you actually come out to Hamilton?

Yes. Hamilton is in Marion County and it's inside our normal working area out of Berry. There's no travel surcharge for being in the eight counties we cover.

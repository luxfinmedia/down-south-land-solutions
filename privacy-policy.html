Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

Down South Land Solutions LLC ("we," "us") operates this website. This page explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it. It is written to be read, not to be impressive.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in the estimate form or call or text, we collect your name, phone number, email address if you give one, the county your property is in, the service you need, and whatever you tell us about the job. We collect this because we cannot price work without it.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, ours records basic technical information — the pages visited, the approximate region a visit came from, the type of device and browser, and the site that referred you. This is aggregate traffic data used to understand which pages are useful. It is not used to identify you personally.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies. Essential cookies make the site work and remember your cookie choice. Analytics cookies, if you accept them, count visits and page views in aggregate. You can decline analytics cookies using the banner, and you can clear cookies at any time in your browser settings. Declining does not limit any part of the site.

How we use your information

To respond to your enquiry, prepare an estimate, schedule and carry out work, invoice for it, and keep ordinary business records. If you become a customer we may contact you about the job itself. We do not add you to a marketing list because you asked for a quote.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We do not trade or rent it. We share it only with service providers who help us operate — for example the company that hosts this website or handles our enquiry form — and only to the extent they need it to provide that service. We will disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become jobs are kept for a reasonable period in case you come back, then discarded. Records relating to work carried out are kept as long as ordinary business and tax record-keeping requires.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call or text (205) 535-6376 or use the contact form and say what you want done. If you asked us to contact you and have changed your mind, tell us and we will stop.

Text messages

If you text us or give us a number, we may reply by text about your enquiry or job. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Reply STOP at any time and we will not text you again.

Children

This site is intended for adults arranging work on property. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS and we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

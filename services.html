Services

Everything we do with a machine

Thirteen services, one crew, eight counties. If what you need isn't here, ask — it's usually still a yes.

  • Forestry Mulching

    One machine, one pass. Standing brush, briars, saplings and small timber go into the head and come out as mulch on the ground — no piles to burn, nothing to haul, and the dirt left covered when we pull off.

  • Land Clearing

    When it's bigger than a mulcher can take, the dozer goes to work. Trees pushed, roots raked out, debris windrowed and burned or hauled depending on what you want left behind.

  • Excavating

    Footings, trenching, culvert sets, stump and root removal, and the jobs that just need a machine on site and somebody who knows how to run it.

  • Site Work

    Access cut in, culverts set, ditching and drainage run so water goes where you want it, laydown areas built, spoil moved and spread. The work nobody thinks about until the concrete truck can't get back there.

  • House Demolition

    Old houses, mobile homes, barns and outbuildings taken down and cleaned up — slab broken out, footprint backfilled and graded, and the lot left so you'd never know anything stood there.

  • Retaining Walls

    Block and boulder walls to hold a cut, flatten a yard that never was flat, or keep a bank out of the driveway every time it rains hard.

  • House Pads

    Cut, filled and compacted to grade so your slab crew shows up to something they can work on instead of something they have to fix first.

  • Underbrush Removal

    Take the junk out and leave the timber standing. Privet, briars, greenbrier, kudzu, honeysuckle and sapling growth cleared out from under trees you want to keep.

  • Hunting Lanes & Food Plots

    Shooting lanes cut, trails opened, food plots cleared and the ground left ready to disc and plant. Hunting land work, done by somebody who understands what it's for.

  • Pasture Reclamation

    Fields that have grown up in sweetgum, briars and privet, brought back to ground you can run cattle on or cut hay off again.

  • Fence Line Clearing

    Fence lines, survey lines and rights of way cut straight, clean and wide enough to actually work in.

  • Firebreaks

    Breaks cut around homes, shops, timber stands and property lines — before you need them, not during.

  • Driveways & Access Roads

    Drives and access roads cut in, based, crowned and ditched so the water leaves instead of running down the middle of them.

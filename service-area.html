Service Area

Eight counties, 34 towns

We run out of Berry in Fayette County. On the edge of this map or just outside it? Call anyway — we'd rather tell you yes or no than have you guess.

Fayette County

Rolling timber country broken up by pasture and small farms. Most of it is mixed pine and hardwood on ground that falls away toward creek bottoms, and a lot of the pasture has been out of use long enough to have grown up.

FayetteBerryBelkGlen Allen

Walker County

Steep hollows, ridge lines and reclaimed mine ground, with lake lots along the Smith Lake side and a lot of former mine property now being sold off in tracts.

JasperCordovaSumitonCarbon HillParrishDora

Tuscaloosa County

Two different jobs in one county — subdivision lots, commercial pads and drainage work around Tuscaloosa and Northport, and timber tracts, hunting land and pasture once you get out past the city.

TuscaloosaNorthportMoundvilleCottondaleBrookwoodCoker

Marion County

Hill country with a lot of standing timber, small pasture tracts and river bottom. Poultry houses and cattle are common, and so is land that has been in timber long enough to need real clearing rather than mulching.

HamiltonWinfieldGuinHackleburg

Lamar County

Quiet, heavily timbered farm country with few towns and a lot of family land. Long driveways, big tracts, overgrown fence lines and pasture coming back out of brush are the everyday work here.

VernonSulligentMillport

Pickens County

Flatter and wetter than the counties north of it, with bottomland hardwood, catfish and row-crop ground, and hunting tracts along the waterway. Drainage work matters more here than almost anywhere else we go.

AlicevilleGordoReformCarrollton

Cullman County

The most farmed of the eight counties — poultry, cattle and row crop on the plateau — with steep lake property along the Smith Lake side where lot clearing and access roads are constant work.

CullmanHancevilleGood Hope

Winston County

The most rugged ground we work. Steep, wooded, cut through with hollows and creeks, ringed by national forest and lake property. Access roads and steep-slope clearing are most of the job here.

Double SpringsHaleyvilleAddisonArley
